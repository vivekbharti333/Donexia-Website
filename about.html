OUR STORY

A Better Way to
Manage. Engage. Impact.

Donexia was founded with a simple belief – NGOs deserve technology that understands their unique challenges. Managing donors, volunteers, campaigns and communications across multiple tools can be overwhelming and time-consuming.

We built Donexia to change that. Our all-in-one CRM brings everything together in one secure, easy-to-use platform so you can focus on what matters most – serving people and creating impact.
