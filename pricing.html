Donexia Pricing FAQs

Quick answers about billing, plan limits, digital receipts and Enterprise pricing.

Yes. The displayed Starter, Growth and Professional prices are monthly subscription prices. Applicable taxes are extra.

Starter supports up to 5 users and 5,000 records, Growth supports up to 15 users and 25,000 records, Professional supports up to 30 users and 100,000 records, and Enterprise limits are customized.

Starter includes email receipts. Growth, Professional and Enterprise include automatic receipt options through SMS, WhatsApp and email, subject to configured communication services.

Enterprise pricing is customized according to users, record limits, branches, workflows, integrations, onboarding, migration and support requirements.
