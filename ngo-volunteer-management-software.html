Why volunteer management matters for NGOs

Volunteers power field work, events and fundraising, but their details often scatter across personal phones and chat groups. When a coordinator changes or a big event arrives, that informality becomes a bottleneck. A volunteer management system keeps every volunteer's record, hours and assignments in one organized place the whole team can use.

Details Donexia keeps with every volunteer

  • Contact details, skills and interests
  • Availability and preferred activities
  • Hours, activities and contribution history
  • Assignments, events and campaign links
  • Communication and follow-up notes
Volunteer profiles hold personal information. Collect only what you need, share it only with authorized team members, and align your usage with your organization's data protection practices.
