Why NGOs use WhatsApp for donation receipts

Many donors already use WhatsApp as an everyday communication channel. A timely message can confirm that the donation was received and make the digital receipt easier to access, while the NGO keeps an organized operational record.

Communication should remain responsible

NGOs should use appropriate donor consent, approved messaging practices and accurate receipt information. Operational automation should support respectful donor relationships, not create unwanted messaging.

WhatsApp availability and message delivery can depend on your approved messaging setup, templates, consent process and provider terms. Confirm the configuration offered for your Donexia plan during onboarding.
