What is donation management for an NGO?

Donation management is the process of recording contributions, connecting them to donor records, issuing acknowledgements and reviewing fundraising performance. For most Indian NGOs this work still lives in registers and individual spreadsheets — donation management software replaces those with a searchable, shared record the whole team can work from.

Details Donexia keeps with every donation

  • Donor name and contact details
  • Donation amount, date and payment mode
  • Transaction or cheque reference
  • Purpose or campaign
  • Receipt number and delivery history
Donexia supports donation recordkeeping and receipt workflows. Tax treatment, receipt requirements and filing duties depend on current rules and your registration — confirm them with a qualified professional.

Why NGOs digitize donations

  • Fewer missed or duplicated entries
  • Faster donor acknowledgement
  • Clearer cash-and-bank visibility
  • Less year-end consolidation

How Donexia donation management works

A simple flow from receiving a contribution to reporting on it.

Step 1

Record or receive

Add a cash, cheque or UPI donation manually, or collect online through a payment link or gateway. Donor, amount, date and reference are saved together.

Step 2

Receipt goes out automatically

A numbered digital receipt is generated from the saved record and sent to the donor through WhatsApp, SMS or email.

Step 3

Track and report

Donations appear in real-time reports by campaign, donor and date — ready for team reviews and Form 10BD preparation.
