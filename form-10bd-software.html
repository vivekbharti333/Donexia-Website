What is Form 10BD?

Form 10BD is a statement of donations filed by eligible reporting entities under applicable Indian income-tax rules. It is associated with donor-level information and the issuance of Form 10BE certificates. Requirements may change, so NGOs should rely on current official guidance and professional advice.

How organized data supports preparation

A consistent operational record can make it easier to review donor identity information, donation amounts, dates and payment references before statutory preparation. It also reduces dependency on disconnected spreadsheets and individual inboxes.

Donexia supports record organization and internal review. This page does not claim automatic filing with the Income Tax Department. Filing scope, deadlines and required identifiers must be confirmed from official sources or a qualified tax professional.
