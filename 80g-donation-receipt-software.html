What is an 80G donation receipt?

An 80G donation receipt is issued by an eligible Indian charitable organization for a qualifying donation. Accurate receipts and underlying records help donors retain evidence of their contribution and help NGOs maintain an orderly audit trail.

Information NGOs commonly organize

  • Donor name, contact details and identification information where applicable
  • Donation amount, date, payment method and transaction reference
  • NGO name, address and relevant registration details
  • Receipt number and authorized acknowledgement
Donexia supports recordkeeping and receipt workflows. Eligibility, required fields and tax treatment depend on current law and the NGO’s circumstances. Confirm requirements with a qualified tax or legal professional.
