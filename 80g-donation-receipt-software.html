80G receipt essentials

What is an 80G donation receipt?

An 80G donation receipt is issued by an eligible Indian charitable organization for a qualifying donation. It gives the donor a record of the contribution and helps the NGO maintain an orderly audit trail.

Exact eligibility, required fields and tax treatment can vary with current rules and an NGO’s circumstances.

A practical note Donexia supports recordkeeping and receipt workflows; it does not replace tax or legal advice. Confirm current requirements with a qualified professional.
