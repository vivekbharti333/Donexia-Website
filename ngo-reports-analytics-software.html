Why reporting matters for NGOs

Funders ask where the money went, trustees ask what is working, and teams ask where to focus next. When answers live in scattered sheets, every question becomes a small project. A reporting system turns your operational records into answers on demand, using the same data your team already enters during daily work.

Reports your team can review in Donexia

  • Donation summaries by period, campaign and payment mode
  • Donor contribution history and repeat-donor visibility
  • Campaign performance against goals
  • Custom views for reviews and presentations (higher plans)
Reports reflect the information recorded in your Donexia account. Figures used for statutory filings or audited statements should be reviewed with your accountant or a qualified professional.
