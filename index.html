Yes. Donexia generates numbered digital donation receipts that capture the fields Indian donors need for Section 80G deductions — donor name, PAN, amount, date and purpose — and sends them automatically by WhatsApp, SMS or email.

Yes. Every donation recorded in Donexia is stored with donor, amount, date and payment details, so your Form 10BD donation records stay organized all year instead of being assembled from spreadsheets in February.
