Receipts that send themselves

Manual receipt work means retyping the same details, chasing delivery and losing track of what was sent. Automation removes all three.

Receipts in seconds, not days

Each receipt is generated from the saved donation record — no retyping donor names, amounts or dates.

Reach donors on any channel

Deliver receipts through WhatsApp, SMS or email, so donors receive acknowledgements where they actually read them.

A complete paper trail

Every receipt is numbered and stored against its donation, ready for retrieval, review and reporting.

Why manual receipts hold NGOs back

When receipts are typed one by one, acknowledgements go out late or not at all, donors follow up twice, and receipt books become hard to audit. Automatic receipts change the default: every donation gets a consistent, numbered acknowledgement without depending on someone's spare evening.

What Donexia stores with every receipt

  • Receipt number and date
  • Donor and donation details
  • Delivery channel and status
  • Linked donation and campaign
